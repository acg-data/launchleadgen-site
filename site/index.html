09/FAQ

Straight answers.

Are the leads really exclusive?

Yes. Each homeowner request goes to one contractor in that territory. We don't resell it, split it or post it to a marketplace.

What counts as a drainage or irrigation lead?

A homeowner in your ZIP codes asking for help with a specific job: French drains, regrading, downspouts, sump pumps, dry wells, sprinkler installs, repairs, drip conversions, controllers, start-ups and winterizations. You pick which job types you want.

How fast do leads arrive?

Leads are pushed by text and email the moment the homeowner finishes the request, and can post straight into most CRMs. Calling in the first few minutes makes a real difference to booking rates, so we build for speed.

What if a lead is bad?

Wrong numbers, duplicates, renters without authority and addresses outside your territory are credited back. Flag it from the lead notification and we review it within one business day.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. Plans are month to month. We ask for a short ramp-up window so campaigns can learn your market, then you can pause or cancel any time.

How quickly can I start?

Most territories go live in about a week: a short onboarding call, ZIP and job-type setup, routing to your phone or CRM, then campaigns launch.
