How it works

We find the homeowners. You win the jobs.

Launch Lead Gen is a done-for-you lead engine for drainage and irrigation contractors: local ads, landing pages, screening and instant delivery, all built around your territory.

The engine

Four steps, running every day.

1

Pick your turf

Choose the ZIP codes, job types and weekly volume you want. Want French drains but not sump pumps? Done.

2

We launch local campaigns

Search, social and landing pages written for homeowners with soggy yards and broken sprinklers, in your area only.

3

Every request is screened

We verify the phone number, confirm the address is in your territory, and capture the problem, timeline and photos.

4

Your phone buzzes

The lead lands by text, email and your CRM in under a minute, so you can call while they're still thinking about it.

Lead quality promise

Six filters before a lead ever reaches you.

Cheap leads are expensive when half of them are junk. Here's what every request has to pass.

Phone verification

Every number is checked before the lead is sent. Disconnected and fake numbers never reach you.

Territory matching

Addresses are matched to your ZIP codes. Out-of-area requests are dropped, not dumped on you.

Ownership screening

We ask if they own the home or are authorized to approve the work.

Intent questions

Problem, scope, timeline and photos, so you can tell a $300 repair from a $9,000 drain job before you call.

One contractor only

No reselling, no splitting, no auction. The lead is yours.

Credits for bad leads

Wrong numbers, duplicates, renters and out-of-area addresses are credited after review.

Onboarding

Live in about a week.

Day 1

Strategy call

We map your ZIP codes, job types, crew capacity, average ticket and the jobs you don't want.

Days 2–4

Build & routing

Local landing pages, ad campaigns and screening questions go up. Leads get routed to your phone, inbox and CRM.

Days 5–7

Go live

Campaigns launch with a weekly lead cap you control. First leads typically land in the first days.

Ongoing

Tune & scale

Tell us which leads closed. We shift budget toward the ZIPs and job types that make you the most money.
