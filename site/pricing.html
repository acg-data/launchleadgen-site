02/FAQ

Pricing questions

Why does the price change by market?

Lead cost depends on local competition, population density and the job types you pick. We quote your exact per-lead rate after we look at your ZIP codes. The prices above are starting points.

Is there a setup fee or contract?

No setup fee on Launch and Growth, and plans are month to month. Territory Lock has a monthly territory fee because we stop selling those ZIP codes to anyone else.

How do I pay?

Pre-fund a lead balance or pay a monthly invoice, whichever suits your cash flow. You're only billed for leads that pass our filters.

Can I pause during the off-season?

Yes. Pause or lower your weekly cap any time. Many irrigation contractors in colder regions pause in winter or shift budget to drainage.

What does a bad-lead credit cover?

Wrong or disconnected numbers, duplicates, renters without authority and addresses outside your territory. Flag it from the notification and we review it within one business day.
