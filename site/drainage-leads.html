01/Job types

Choose the drainage jobs you want.

Turn each job type on or off. We only advertise for, screen and send the work that fits your crews and equipment.

French drain installation

Backyard pooling, wet lawns and runoff toward the house. Usually the largest drainage job we send.

Yard grading & regrading

Negative slope, low spots and erosion. Often bundled with drains or downspout work.

Downspout & gutter drainage

Buried extensions, pop-up emitters and tie-ins. Smaller jobs that often lead to larger ones.

Sump pumps & basement drainage

Wet basements, failed pumps and interior drains. Usually urgent, especially after storms.

Dry wells & catch basins

Common on clay soils and small lots where water has nowhere to drain.

Channel & trench drains

Driveways, patios and garage entries that flood every time it rains.

What every lead has to pass.

  • Inside your territoryAddress checked against the ZIP codes you chose.
  • Verified phone numberNo fake numbers, no bots, no test submissions.

Bad-lead credit policy

If a lead slips through with a wrong number, a duplicate, a renter or an out-of-area address, flag it from the notification. We review it within one business day and credit it back.

06/FAQ

Drainage Leads: common questions

Do you send foundation repair or waterproofing leads?

We focus on exterior and basement drainage. If a homeowner mentions structural cracks or major waterproofing, we flag it in the lead so you can decide whether to take it or refer it.

Can I exclude certain job types?

Yes. Many crews skip sump pumps or interior drains. Choose the drainage jobs you want and we only advertise for and send those.

What happens after a big storm?

Drainage requests usually rise after heavy rain. Your weekly cap keeps volume at what your crews can handle, and we can raise it for a few weeks if you want more.

How do you verify drainage leads?

Every phone number is verified, the address is checked against your territory, and the homeowner answers questions about the problem, the timeline and whether they own the home.
