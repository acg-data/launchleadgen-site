Job types

Choose the drainage jobs you actually want.

Turn each job type on or off. We only target, screen and send the work that fits your crews and equipment.

French drain installation

Backyard pooling, soggy lawns and runoff toward the house. Usually the highest-ticket request we send.

Yard grading & regrading

Negative slope, low spots and erosion. Often bundled with drains or downspout work.

Downspout & gutter drainage

Buried extensions, pop-up emitters and tie-ins. Quick wins that open the door to bigger jobs.

Sump pumps & basement drainage

Wet basements, failed pumps and interior drains. Urgent, high-intent and storm-driven.

Dry wells & catch basins

Where water needs somewhere to go. Common in clay soils and tight lots.

Channel & trench drains

Driveways, patios and garage entries that flood every time it rains.

FAQ

Drainage Leads: common questions

Do you send foundation repair or waterproofing leads?

We focus on exterior and basement drainage. If a homeowner mentions structural cracks or major waterproofing, we flag it in the lead so you can decide whether to refer it.

Can I exclude certain job types?

Yes. Many crews skip sump pumps or interior drains. Pick exactly the drainage jobs you want and we'll only target and send those.

What happens after a big storm?

Drainage requests usually spike after heavy rain. You set a weekly cap so you never get more leads than your crews can handle, and we can raise it temporarily if you want the surge.

How do you verify drainage leads?

Every phone number is verified, the address is checked against your territory, and the homeowner answers screening questions about the problem, the timeline and whether they own the home.
