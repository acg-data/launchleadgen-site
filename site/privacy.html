Privacy Policy

Last updated October 13, 2026

What we collect

When you use the forms on this site we collect what you enter: your name, company, email, phone number, the ZIP codes you serve, the lead types and plan you're interested in, and any message. Like most websites, our hosting provider may log basic technical data such as your IP address and browser type.

How we use it

We use your details to respond to your request, check territory availability, prepare a quote and, if you become a client, deliver and support the service. We may contact you by phone, text or email about your request. You can opt out of texts by replying STOP, and out of emails using the link in any message.

Sharing

We share your details only with service providers that help us run the business (for example email, CRM and hosting providers) and when required by law. We do not sell the contact details you submit through this site.

Third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads. If we add analytics or advertising tools, we'll list them here.

Your choices

To access, correct or delete the information you've sent us, email hello@launchleadgen.io.

Changes

We'll post any updates to this policy on this page with a new date.
