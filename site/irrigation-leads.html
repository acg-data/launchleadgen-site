06/FAQ

Irrigation Leads: straight answers

Do you send commercial irrigation leads?

Our campaigns target homeowners. Occasionally a small commercial or HOA request comes through; we label it clearly and you can opt in or out.

Can I get only repair leads, or only installs?

Yes. Choose any mix: installs, repairs, seasonal service or all of it. Many companies start with repairs because they close fast and lead to service plans.

Will I get leads in winter?

Irrigation slows in the off-season in colder climates. That's why many contractors pair irrigation with drainage, which picks up with fall and late-winter rain.

How do you verify irrigation leads?

Every phone number is verified, the address is checked against your territory, and the homeowner tells us what they need, the system size and their timeline.
